# How to use CrossWise

CrossWise uses the phone camera to say what it sees on the road. It is a research aid. It never says a road is safe. You decide when to cross, and a sighted helper should be with you while you learn it.

Some behaviour below is planned and is being built now.

## Before you start
- Charge the phone. Keep the volume up.
- Use one earbud or bone-conduction headphones. Keep one ear free for traffic.
- Allow camera, microphone and location when Android asks.
- Hold the phone upright at chest height, camera facing out. Keep your fingers off the lens.
- Practise first. In Settings, choose "Practise the check". It runs the whole turn at home. No cars are checked.
- The first start after installing takes about 10 seconds. The app says "Getting ready", then plays a "ready" tone. Later starts take about 1 second.

## The check, step by step
1. Stand at the kerb, facing the road.
2. Press the big Start button, or say "start".
3. Turn RIGHT until the phone points up the road, toward the traffic. Hold still for 5 seconds. You hear a soft tick each second, then "Right side checked".
4. Now turn all the way round to the LEFT, past the road, until the phone points down the road. Hold still again.
5. Face the road. The phone gives its result.

The phone learns the angle of the road from passing cars. If it sees none, it uses a right angle and says "Couldn't see the road's angle, using straight left."

## What the phone says and what to do
- "No vehicles seen on either side. Cross if you judge it clear." The camera saw nothing. That is not a promise. Listen and judge for yourself.
- "Vehicle on your right, moving. Wait." The same can be said for your left. Do not cross. Check again.
- "Vehicle seen, not moving. Check again." A parked or stopped vehicle may start. Check again.
- "Camera could not see clearly. Check again."

A result runs out after 20 seconds. If you wait longer, check again.

## Crossing
After a result you have two buttons: "Check again", then "Cross".
- Press "Cross" only if you judge it clear.
- The phone says "About N steps". N comes from the road width in Settings.
- It calls the halfway point, then "2 steps left".
- It warns you if a car comes toward you.
- At the end it says "Use your cane to find the kerb."

## When something goes wrong
- Too dark, or the camera sees badly: the phone says "Camera could not see clearly. Check again." Do not cross on that check.
- You turned the wrong way first: the phone tells you to keep turning or turn back a little. Follow it, or say "stop" and start again.
- You stopped turning: the phone prompts you. If nothing changes, it gives up and asks you to check again.
- Camera off: the screen says "Camera is off". Allow the camera in Android Settings, Apps, CrossWise, Permissions. Never cross without a working camera.
- Screen locked or power button pressed while crossing: stop, use your own senses, and check again from the kerb.
- If you are unsure at any time, say "stop" and ask your helper.

## Settings
- Hold time: 3 to 8 seconds. The default is 5.
- Road width: 1 to 4 lanes. Each lane is about 6 steps.
- Speech, tones, vibration and speech rate.
- Steering hints: experimental. They are off by default.
- Developer mode: for testers only. It shows timings.

## Voice commands
Say: start, check again, cross, stop, repeat, help.
Voice needs offline English speech recognition on the phone. Wind and traffic noise can hide your voice. The buttons always work.

## Safety limits
- CrossWise says what the camera saw. It cannot say a crossing is safe.
- It can miss vehicles, quiet electric cars, bikes and people, especially at night, in rain, or if the view is blocked.
- It does not read traffic lights.
- Use your cane, your hearing and your training. Your helper or trainer decides.
- Never rely on the app alone.
