Motion experiments — current detector held fixed
1. Baseline exact mobile JS algorithm: one affine background transform plus object flow/box histories.
2. Improvement attempt 1: gamma enhancement before sparse flow.
3. Attempt 2: homography instead of affine and relaxed background spatial gates.
4. Attempt 3: stronger temporal voting and removal of bbox-only fallback.
5. After three attempts: cloned and executed Norfair camera-motion estimator, combined with OpenCV forward/back LK and robust fundamental-matrix residuals.
6. Ground-region homography with bottom-centre history.
7. Replayed all recordings with the BDD detector and unchanged motion algorithm.

Results of point audit, current detector:
Baseline: parked 4 incorrectly moving / 27 uncertain / 1 missed; moving 1 correctly moving / 8 uncertain / 4 missed.
Attempt 1: parked 2 incorrectly moving; moving 1 correctly moving.
Attempt 2: parked 13 incorrectly moving; moving 1 correctly moving.
Attempt 3: parked 7 incorrectly moving; moving 0 correctly moving.
Norfair + epipolar: no parked points incorrectly moving, but all detected moving points uncertain. This is conservative abstention, not successful motion recognition.
Ground homography: parked 7 incorrectly moving; moving 0 correctly moving.
All variants retain uncertainty in poor texture; none proves parked status on this walking night sample. Detailed audit-summary.json includes misses and uncertain counts, plus BDD full-replay point audit.

Why it fails
A walking camera makes near and far stationary objects move by different amounts. One affine/homography cannot explain a non-planar street. Bounding-box shifts add detector jitter and identity changes. Low light and glare remove trackable texture. Epipolar inliers are not proof of stationarity: vehicles can move along epipolar lines. A better detector helps locate vehicles, but is not a replacement for ego-motion/depth evidence.

App changes
- BDD nano 640 is bundled as the new default for unset model selections; explicit custom model choices are preserved. The connected phone was explicitly selected to use a copy of the same model with CPU backend for diagnosis.
- Seven labels mapped, including rider -> person. This model has NO crosswalk or traffic-signal classes; it must not be treated as such a model.
- Walking IMU state now forces vehicle motion and direction to UNCERTAIN and resets history. Detections/uncertain alerts remain. Stationary evidence must be rebuilt after stopping. This avoids known false motion claims, but is not a solution for walking-camera motion classification. The recorded replay has no IMU and does not simulate this gate.
- Developer moving / stationary / uncertain box icons, selected states and slashes; persistent settings; independent of alerts. Defaults show moving and uncertain only. Also accessible under Settings > Vehicle boxes.
- Developer track list shows motion and direction.
- Camera session failure, waiting for inference, slow inference, and inference errors are separate states. Real native error and timing are visible in Developer diagnostics. Frames older than 350 ms still cannot guide the user.
- Explicit starting screen, camera interruption resume fix, resized-buffer release on exceptions.
- Hardened native model weight ownership for imported JS buffers through scripts/patch-tflite-lifetime.cjs and postinstall. Borrowed JS model bytes now copy to native-owned memory. This fixes a lifetime hazard; the existing device crash is not proven to have this cause.
