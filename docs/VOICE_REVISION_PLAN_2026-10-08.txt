CrossWise speech revision plan — 8 October 2026
Status: proposed; source audit completed. This document does not claim implementation or phone validation.
Target: /Users/hieu/Desktop/capstone/capstone_ios (shared React Native source).

1. Speech policy
Every spoken message must serve an observation, immediate instruction, action confirmation, or actionable failure. Prefer one short sentence. Usually 2–8 words; preserve necessary meaning rather than enforcing a word limit. No branding, greetings, software state narration, repeated command tutorials, rhetorical questions, or generic caution paragraphs during daily use. Speak only facts supported by current inputs. Keep explanations and commands in Settings > Voice help and Manual. Make short speech and its caption agree. Screen-reader access to controls and error recovery remains available.

2. Startup and listening
Remove VOICE_WELCOME in full, including “Welcome to CrossWise” and “After the tone”. Remove the forced-restart sentence “After the tone, say navigate to a place, or manual.” Auto listening continues after permissions and actual microphone readiness. Use a distinct brief listening cue and optional haptic when audio capture is actually ready. Do not reuse the CENTERED navigation/signal chime. Silent timeout/rearming stays silent; no welcome or listening explanation on each restart or after a vehicle alert. Explain the listening cue in Manual and let users preview it. Respect tones/haptics settings; expose Listening / Working / Voice unavailable as accessible status. A normal app launch must not announce model loading, location checks or app internals. A real permission/recognition failure is an exception and needs one brief actionable message.

4. Vehicle phrases
Vehicle detected. -> keep for uncertain motion. It must not be relabelled as approaching.
Vehicle coming from the left/right. -> keep only with usable motion direction and reliable phone orientation; suppress side claims during turning or stale orientation.
Vehicle approaching. -> reserve for evidence of approach. If only movement is established and approach is unknown: “Moving vehicle detected.” Horizontal motion alone does not establish closing distance or collision risk.
Vehicle close, left/ahead/right. -> do not claim measured distance from image size alone. Use “Caution. Vehicle from the left/right.” only with supported direction, otherwise “Caution. Vehicle detected.” If later calibrated proximity becomes available, distance wording can be reconsidered.
[number] approaching vehicle(s) detected. -> replace with evidence-aware status: “[number] moving vehicles” and/or “[number] vehicles with uncertain motion.” Never count all hazards as confirmed approach.
No moving vehicles detected. (Repeat) -> “No moving vehicles in view.” Only with fresh usable frames, enabled/working detection and no unresolved vehicle uncertainty. Otherwise “Traffic assessment unavailable” or “Vehicle detected. Motion uncertain.” Disabling alerts must never create a false clear report.
Keep vibration for warnings; coalesce multiple vehicles into one relevant warning instead of overlapping per-object speech. Stationary vehicles remain silent once supported by motion evidence. Do not infer clearance from detector silence.

5. Guided scan
Keep your body facing forward. Point the phone left and hold. -> “Face forward. Point the phone left. Hold still.” Once when a scan starts; explain phone-only turning in Manual.
Point the phone right and hold. -> “Point the phone right. Hold still.” Only when valid left-side evidence has completed.
Scan complete. No moving vehicles detected. -> keep as a completed observation summary, once, never “Cross now”. Requires fresh usable evidence from both sides and no unresolved moving/uncertain vehicle or blocking signal.
Interrupted scan -> hazard warning first; after the warning and once rescan is possible, “Scan again. Point the phone left.” Do not silently invalidate the scan while the user waits on the right. Distinguish a first scan from a restart.
Bad aim -> “Raise the phone.” / “Lower the phone.” Only after sustained incorrect positioning and verified sensor-to-camera mapping. Do not prompt repeatedly during an intentional scan movement.
Missing/stale/dark camera -> one specific failure, e.g. “Camera view too dark.” / “Camera blocked.” / “Traffic assessment unavailable.” Never produce a clear-scan result from unusable frames. Avoid an unending hold with no explanation.

Assistance off. -> “Camera help off.” Only after explicit user disable; do not announce it redundantly during backgrounding, route pause or navigation stop.
Crossing mode on. / Crossing detected. -> “Crossing guidance started.” Only when the guidance state really begins; never present this as proof that a road crossing was visually recognized.
Crossing mode off. -> “Crossing guidance ended.” Only on explicit completed-crossing action, with route resumption handled by one next instruction. No duplicate mode and route announcements.
Crossing mode on. Direction guidance unavailable. -> “Direction guidance unavailable.” Once for an actual failure of an enabled capability.
Bear left/right. / On course. / Drifted left/right. -> withhold claims of walking direction from phone heading alone. Turning the phone during a scan is not body drift. Before restoring these phrases, establish that heading is a valid walking-direction estimate. Do not solve this by confidently renaming inaccurate steering advice.
Crossing help. Route speech paused. -> “Crossing guidance.” Once on the handoff; explain route speech pause in Manual.

Crossing ended. Journey paused. -> “Crossing guidance ended. Navigation paused.” Do not claim the person has reached the footpath merely because guidance is disabled.

Point camera left/ahead/right. (Gemini) -> “Point the phone left/forward/right.” Use the same terminology as vehicle scan; explain purpose in optional Describe help.

10. Delivery rules and known trigger defects
- Centralize all speech text, including strings currently embedded in controller/nativeSpeech/navigation; define ID, trigger, priority, repetition policy, capability and sample values.
- Traffic warnings and loss of traffic assessment outrank route directions, scan prompts, confirmations and help. Deferred route/scan speech must be checked against current state before playback; discard stale items.
- A fresh hazard cancels a queued no-vehicle/scan-complete observation. A scan completion cannot outlive its supporting evidence or play after a camera/model interruption.
- Deduplicate identical events across controller, Journey and voice handler. No repeated startup lines after TTS/listening handoffs. No searching/planning/saved confirmation interrupting a hazard.
- Start scan timers/instructions in the correct order. The user must hear the required action; if urgent speech suppresses an instruction, retry it once appropriate, rather than advancing the scan without it.
- Restrict route-distance-loss announcements to active journeys. Separate intentional route pause from degraded GPS.
- Keep one restrained listening cue; avoid playing it after every silent recognition timeout. Never begin recognition until app speech has ended. This plan does not add voice interruption/wake-word support.
- Track counts in Repeat must reflect motion evidence and detector/alert state; current hazards.length can misrepresent both motion and disabled alerts.
- Manual and Practice must mirror current capabilities. Trained-signal demos are demonstrations, not proof the default COCO detector recognizes pedestrian walk signals.

11. Implementation order
A. Remove welcome and after-the-tone speech, recurring command tutorials and automatic mode announcements; centralize remaining phrases. Keep actual microphone-ready confirmation distinct and accessible.
B. Correct vehicle, status, scan and signal wording/capability gates. Fix interruption/restart prompts and stale observation playback.
C. Simplify destination search, route instructions, saved-place feedback and failure messages; eliminate duplicate confirmations. Resolve completion-command mismatch before speaking commands.
D. Update Manual, captions and Practice. Add Developer > Speech preview: every phrase with sample values, trigger and Play / Stop; optional matching tone/haptic; Play all and Next for a controlled review. Preview must be isolated from live route/perception commands.
E. Verify timing and transitions, then build/install on the iPhone. Road perception accuracy remains a separate evaluation.

12. Acceptance checks
Opening app: no welcome, branding, after-the-tone, model-loaded or routine location narration; microphone cue only when genuinely ready. Silence/rearm and return from Settings/background do not restart tutorials.
Search: one result, multiple indistinguishable results, no result, delayed request, cancel, duplicate save, route failure. Every spoken command must work in the current context.
Route: start once, Repeat, pause/resume once, inaccurate GPS once, recovery, off-route, nearby destination, explicit arrival. No wrong step or automatic crossing/arrival claim.
Traffic: moving left/right/unknown direction, uncertain motion, stationary vehicle, multiple vehicles, alert disabled, unsupported/dark/stale camera. No false clear report.
Scan: left hold, right hold, scan interruption, prompt preemption, camera failure and later hazard. No stale completion played after a hazard.
Signals: supported pedestrian classes versus generic COCO lights; lost signal during and outside crossing. No generic light announced as verified pedestrian permission.
Phone/body distinction: turning the phone alone must not produce “Bear left/right” steering.
Speech preview: all fixed phrases and dynamic examples covered; no real route/device command executed by Play; Stop cancels queue; preferences and accessibility remain effective.
Run focused behavioral tests for these changes plus repository lint/typecheck and existing regression suite; test microphone/listening cue and warning interruption on the actual iPhone. Record physical checks separately from unit tests. Do not claim generated fixture tests establish outdoor detection accuracy.
